# Wanderer v0.13 — Optional Contracts: seven objectives and extraction-secured rewards

## Decision

**The chosen rule is implemented: completing the objective DOES NOT pay immediately.** A Contract pays **once**, only when the player extracts alive or defeats the final Boss. An objective completed before death or abandonment earns **no Contract prize**. Other resources normally gained from combat remain governed by their own game rules.

The **existing two Contract choices per Expedition are retained**; neither is mandatory.

## Seven real native objectives

| Contract | Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Bounty Writ | Defeat the marked Elite | Native marked-Elite completion |
| Pathfinder | Clear encounters in two distinct map sectors | Cleared rooms and native controller history |
| Purification | Disable a Boss property in the Forgotten Reliquary | Native Reliquary ledger |
| Oath of Defiance | Defeat the Boss without disabling a Boss property | Native final Boss state |
| **Monster Hunt** | Defeat **6 / 8 / 10** normal monsters (Guarded / Dangerous / Brutal) | Actual native completed ordinary-wave enemy count recorded in `game.history[].monstersDefeated` |
| **Skirmish Orders** | Clear three normal combat rooms | Distinct truly cleared ordinary combat rooms |
| **Shrine Oath** | Select a Boon at a discovered Shrine | Native `choose_boon` decision on a reachable Shrine |

Each offer is determined deterministically from the actual map and is tested for reachability within the Expedition step budget. Scouting alone, unfinished fights, and Boss/Elite kills never count toward Monster Hunt. No quest progress is generated by merely choosing a Contract.

## Exclusive variable rewards

Each Contract has a deterministic seeded basic prize (the two offers use different basic prize types):
- **Gold:** 28 / 45 / 70 for Guarded / Dangerous / Brutal.
- **Essence:** 4 / 7 / 11.
- **Fragments:** 8 / 14 / 23.
- **Reagent:** 1 / 1 / 2 for the selected Realm's Affinity.
- **Potion:** one real existing Ironhide, Purifying or Fate potion, chosen from the seeded reward plan.

Every offered Contract may independently display a **Blueprint chance: 20% / 30% / 40%** based on difficulty, provided that an undiscovered matching Rare/Epic crafting recipe exists. Each of the two offers reserves a different eligible recipe; no duplicate Blueprint is offered, and once all applicable recipes are known, only the guaranteed basic prize is offered. The Blueprint **replaces** the basic reward if won; if the chance misses, the basic prize is paid. No double rewards or conversion of duplicate known Blueprints into currency. The reward preview displays the exact candidate recipe and its fallback.

## Correct settlement and replay validation

- `online/v012-persistence-runtime.js` passes the explicit outcome `extracted`; `online/v012-expedition-runtime.js` passes `victory` only after a completed Boss.
- `online/v013-expedition-contracts.js` refuses to pay without the appropriate successful outcome **and correct native controller stage**, regardless of earlier objective completion.
- Every actual payout records one receipt `stage7RewardPaid`; double settlements return null. Its kind, amounts, selected recipe and canonical deterministic plan are validated on checkpoint restoration. Death/abandon does not settle pending rewards.
- `combat/v013-expedition-foundation.js` lists the three new IDs, with no other Catalog changes.
- The normal combat runtime records actual killed-mob counts **only after wave victory**. This is not a general mob rebalance and does not affect Boss or Elite stats.
- `index.html` and the isolated `docs/` Pages preview display the extraction-only and death-forfeiture rules with correct per-Contract reward previews. Mirrored modules are byte-identical with canonical gameplay sources and use updated asset versions.
- No existing save migration is needed or claimed; v0.13 is a clean-sheet development game. Historical optional Contract offer plans from earlier revisions are not supported by this revised deterministic plan.
