# B1 — General E10 rebaseline (Seventh Reach)
Current consolidated branch after S4.3 (d726f63, 655/655 regression executions). User chose to defer art production to Godot and resume mechanical balance before migration. No gameplay coefficients, drop rates or talent rules changed here.

Two separate tracks:
- Controlled: 37 build variants (22 pure/weapon/element variants, 15 affinity pairs: six actual mixed axes plus nine combinations without dedicated mixed skills), 20 scenarios per build, 50 shared combat seeds per scenario = 37,000 isolated E10 encounters. Group sizes 1,4,8,12,16,22; eight named elite types; six normal bosses. Native Rare gear at E10, legal 30-point allocation, no runes or potions. This is diagnostic, not earned progression; compare actual roster hashes and requested vs actual sizes, retain timeouts. Do not equate aggregate unweighted controlled rates to natural win rates.
- Natural: 28 selected current agents/profiles, 50 independent accounts each (1,400 accounts planned); E1 start, no gifted gear, XP or unlocks; max E10, 12 attempts per E and 60 total attempts per account. Native crafting, merchant, potions, runes, boons, contracts, risk selection, retreat, and adaptive targeting. Fixed initial profile identities prevent unrelated explicit profile pivots; strategic adaptive third-affinity plans remain legal. Natural agent choices and actual weapon/armor/affinity/element may differ; report rather than force. Accounts not reaching E10 remain in reach statistics and never become synthetic E10 snapshots.
- Read-only telemetry: actual live talent IDs/names, ability activation counters, observed AP/SP and mana, damage, HP, healing, barriers, group visibility, potion acquisition/use/loadout and materials, Forge recipes and influence, contract payouts. Remove retired lightning_focus from duplicated AoE classification and use current classifier. Counters do not cover every passive or entry trigger; zero observed activations are a diagnosis, not proof of failure. No large balance changes before reading results.
- Reference natural E10 aggregate targets from the shared v013-balance-criteria.cjs: normal 85%, elite 70%, boss 50%; ideal ±5 percentage points; acceptable ±12.5 percentage points (25 pp total). Acceptable bands: normal 72.5–97.5%, elite 57.5–82.5%, boss 37.5–62.5%. Report encounter-weighted aggregate natural E10 separately; compare by type and size, not impose identical rates on each build. These bands are diagnostic only. Controlled encounters remain separate.
