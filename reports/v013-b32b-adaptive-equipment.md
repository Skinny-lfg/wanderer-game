New adaptive and adaptiveSoft use the same fixed expected encounter mix (25% solo, 40% group, 35% boss; design assumptions, not natural frequencies). Score only projected precombat native stats, real offered gear and acquired nodes. Native offhand rate from raw speed and speed flat, exact weapon coefficients and conditional cleave gates, approximate resource envelope (60s) and expected status proc frequency under shared ICDs and mana. Approximate Rot and Bleed tick contribution, Guard/evasion/defense mitigation at fixed reference enemy attack 30/55, modest Barrier/healing sustain proxy. No claim to predict full ability dynamics, true DPS, exact TTK or actual resource payment. Native effect rates are approximated with explicitly declared horizons, cap and availability assumptions. Soft candidate adds bounded 0.035 log-score preference for the current plan's compatible weapon, scaled by earned points. No hard exclusion or fictitious gear. Both candidates rank a whole hand set once, not choose different sets for a known future room.
