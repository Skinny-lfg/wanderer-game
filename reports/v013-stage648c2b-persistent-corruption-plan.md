# Stage 6.48C.2B — Persistent Corruption (research only)

Four paired VM-only arms, 320 fights each, same two earned E10 Wand/Tome actors and same seeds, equipment, enemies and target policy as Stage 6.48C.2. Baseline compared with the historic 200 group cells. No production/deployment changes; Blood and enemy coefficients unchanged.

A baseline. B paid primary Toxic Touch Rot persists until explicit consumption, replacement at native cap, death or encounter reset; native free, secondary, spread and transferred Rot still expires. C B plus Cycle check after Toxic Touch. D C plus Cycle consumes the three oldest stacks instead of one; provisional burst = two ticks per consumed primary stack (secondary capped by actual remaining ticks), plus existing 20% Spell Power. Existing 4 Mana cost, five-second cooldown and three-stack threshold unchanged. B/C keep the original finite application tick budget as a proxy for legacy burst/copy math; this intentionally gives a persistent stack a full finite legacy budget, not an infinite damage multiplier. Frost extension skipped on paid persistent stacks. Lingering Rot's extra tick no longer affects primary sustained lifetime, but can affect finite proxy and transferred stacks. Other talents unchanged; nonowner B/C/D equality checked. No assumption that the D multiplier is balanced.

Metrics include win/loss, mean fight DPS, damage, mana, cycle activations and consumed damage, actual Rot damage (all origins), first cap per target and elapsed combat time, primary expiry, Nova and Soul Drain. Two actors only, only one owns Cycle: not sufficient for full build identity, Blood comparison or hybrid safety. Spore Mark copies remain temporary because primaryPaid is cleared; Contagious Touch, Grave Bloom and Carrion spread use finite copies; Wasting Curse and Wither uptime may increase. Additional mixed-build and early/late level experiments required before adoption. No native files modified.
