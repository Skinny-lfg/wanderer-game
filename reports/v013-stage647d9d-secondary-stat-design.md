Decision: 1/1.5/2% Attack Speed and Mana Regeneration, 2/3/4% Maximum Mana, +2/3/4 percentage points Critical Chance. Evasion Rating stays flat 2/3/4 and its existing level-relative curve. No new nodes or prerequisites; deliberate distribution: Fury 9 Critical Chance and 3 Attack Speed slots, Blood 6 Critical Chance, Magic 3 Critical Chance. Existing primary attributes and roots retained. Exact slot choices are deterministic, with preferences for related gates and existing secondary stats. Previous skill-specific flat Mana and Regen (Arcane) remain. Critical Chance is additive, cap 100% at gear/buildcraft/final stats; existing gear-affix contribution cap of 8 points and diminishing Charisma curve retained. Precision keeps its diminishing rank formula and receives accurate description; normal critical multiplier 1.5 unchanged.

Audit: Aegis lacks ordinary Block Chance nodes although Shield gear and Guarded Block already support blocking; candidate for next review (shield-only stat nodes or explicit nonshield support, cap remains 65%). Barrier has flat encounter start nodes and HP/SP-based affinity; ward gear exists, no general ward multiplier node. Review those before adding more defense. Critical Damage, general Healing Power, Mana Cost Reduction and status application chance are not generic minor stats; related abilities already exist, and these could disproportionately amplify proc/sustain loops. Blood already has Bleed damage/speed, Decay has Rot damage/defense reduction, Fury has Sunder damage, Vitality regen, Magic spell/regen/mana. No additional nodes for these without a separate approved design. Crit synergies include Sanguine Fury, Brutal Critical, Adrenaline, Crimson Rush, Fervent Life, Tempest Talon and Critical Focus/Perfect Strike. Direct critical rolls only; no automatic crit on secondary spell/DoT damage. Test cap, graph allocation, legacy skill compatibility, native progression, regression suite. Planner uses heuristic normalized weights.
