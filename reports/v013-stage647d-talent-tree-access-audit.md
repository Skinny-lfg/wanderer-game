## Proposed symmetrical three lanes per affinity

Use the same visual slots and two prerequisite stat steps (account for skill() also creating a gate; existing weapon forks actually add four nodes). Current six weapon paths plus 12 possible new paths = 415 graph nodes if retaining the same four-node topology. This is not a target requiring twelve additive buffs. Magic elemental modes already share visual coordinates and are alternate, not simultaneously visible. Plan geometric collision/edge checks and real Chromium screenshot before modifying live tree. Maintain point cap 100.

## Implementation gates

1. No gameplay edits in this audit. First layout prototype, preserve graph and old IDs/costs; inspect collisions, edges, readable zoom/fit; do not move visual position alone and imply early access.
2. Pilot low-point legal routes 5/8/12/18 and E10 snapshots with equal earned budget, natural and assisted distinct. Native 100 matched seeds per group/variant, at least 5 independent actors when possible; if insufficient, report it and collect more accounts. Include 1/2/3/4/8/12 enemies (8/12 reinforcements, max 4 active).
3. Target weak pure Vitality, Aegis (Shield/Orb), Decay without mixed, Arcane without Bloom and Frost; controls Blood/Decay Carrion, Fire, Lightning, Plate Warden, Grave Bloom and generic Focused + spells, weapon variants. Compare ability isolated and hybrid interactions, solo/elite/boss damage, time to kill, mana, healing, barrier, secondary damage, activation counts. Avoid changing enemy coefficients and talent power simultaneously.
4. Design guardrails: no new early generic % damage/crit/haste, no double-procs, no free resources per target, cap encounter-level periodic effects, explicit weapon/attunement eligibility; monitor powerful hybrid controls. Proposed non-inferiority check for already strong builds: no large systematic win-rate/TTK buff (e.g. >5 percentage points or >10% faster at same matched gear/budget; sampling uncertainty must be considered), not a guarantee. First test priority candidates, then decide rest, and only then Stage 6.48.
