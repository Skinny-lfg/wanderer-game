## Source audit and simulator updates

- All 18 direct stat types remain native. Sim now reports Critical Chance, Guard chance/strength, Evasion Rating, Mana, Mana Regen, Ward Bonus, Barrier Power, Healing Power and Mana Cost Reduction, with actual combat healing/Barrier/mana/secondary damage and measured event counters. Critical display and Guard log counters are diagnostic, not probabilities or unique attacks.
- Agent classification includes Vital Pulse, Grasping Roots, Ward Echo, native paid Rot spread/nova, Carrion Detonation, Arcane Nova and Frost Resonance, with weapon/element/resource checks and new defense stats. Agent gear/talent valuations use a new opt-in research policy with live Camp stat context, capped-stat awareness, spell/mana activation, conditional Legendary preferences and fixed intended archetypes. Planner remains heuristic, not exhaustive optimization.
- Guard talents enable 35% reduction without Shield. Existing gear blockBonus still applies only with Shield (gear-stats line 112). Therefore some rolled affixes can be inactive on shieldless Guard builds. Candidate: decide whether to rename affix Guard Chance and enable when Guard already active. Do not change before comparison.
- Gear STR/AGI/INT/END/CHA remain active; Magic/Decay enable resource and SP on physical weapons; Aegis/Orb enable defensive SP without Mana. Gear affix pool supports both physical/magical Decay. Offhand Dagger contributes 50% offensive stats and one secondary hit every two primary hits; corrected interpretation for audit. Plate/Shield strength remains a plausible source of opening dominance. Armor weighting and gear availability need controlled E10 evidence, not blanket buffs.
- Crit chance benefits Sanguine Fury/Adrenaline/Brutal Critical/Crimson Rush/Tempest Talon. Normal weapon attacks including funded caster weapon hits roll crit. Automatic extra Magic hits and secondary DoTs are not generally crit-enabled; Magic crit nodes are therefore not a universal spell-damage multiplier. Review whether intended.
- Twelve Legendary signatures reviewed from combat/legendary.js and v012-combat-runtime.js. Veil and Heartroot benefit from new Barrier/Healing generation, but Heartroot keeps 10% realm budget; Eye of Morrow uses pre-crit base hit, conditional Rot and class cap; Chalice benefits Healing Power on bleeding hit; Oathwall requires real Guard; Tempest Talon requires actual critical; Astrafall/Voidglass are funded main-weapon cast triggers, not every Arcane Infusion/automatic spell. No synthetic Legendary items injected; superiority over Epic not established unless naturally available or separately controlled matched equipment comparison. Historical proposed Legendary backlog not implemented here.
