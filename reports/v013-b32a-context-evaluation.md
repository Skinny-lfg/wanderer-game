## Design
12 profiles covering six affinities and one hybrid; fixed legal E10 Rare builds with 30 points. 20 fresh matched seeds 103000001+i*113, solo/group12/boss, 720 decision sets. Available full hand bundles generated natively for each seed, same nonhand gear and roster; no fictitious offhand with two-hand weapons. Each policy chooses from identical bundle set. H23 with fixed baseline stats/owned talents, H23 without planner layer (ablation), blind and encounter-context variants of experimental precombat native-stat ranking. Proxy uses nominal weapon coefficients and bounded mana availability (60s horizon, approximate), damage rate, owned weapon talent cleave, status frequency and durability. Does not use actual encounter outcomes, costs of other abilities, precise haste/defense formula, or hidden enemy details. The context-specific variant sees only scenario category and may be unsuitable when future encounter is unknown. Its weights are predefined heuristic research candidates, not balance changes. Report selection frequency, win/DPS/HP, paired differences and missed winning alternatives. Oracle is hindsight upper bound, not achievable agent performance. Compare candidate vs H23, not equality of each weapon. No actual acquired inventory constraints or merchant/craft pricing in this controlled stage; do not infer real natural progression. Agent profile and fixed controlled talents are contextual proxies; profiles are not earned.
