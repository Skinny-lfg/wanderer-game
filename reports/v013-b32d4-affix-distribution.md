## Experiment

Native Forge prepareCraft with real native makeItem in independent diagnostic harness, synthetic offline resources and known recipe solely to sample distributions. No earned account, no insertion of sampled items, no advance RNG access. 100 rolls per representative recipe/influence/context. Dedup identical type/rarity/trait/influence/slot distributions except separate ring slots; sample intended main weapon, compatible offhands, all intended armor slots, alternative armor types in chest, rings and amulets; all actual available Uncommon/Rare/Epic recipes in scope. Preferred legal influence and uninfluenced; no Common/Legendary Forge recipes, no hypothetical Epic accessories. Other alternate recipe IDs may differ in reagent family availability/cost. Compare native preview with actual affix distribution and best legal slot utility vs frozen incumbent; two ring slots evaluated for every ring. Current B3.2D utility proxy, no combat reruns, not guaranteed combat improvement. E3 talent prefix is legal but not natural history.
