# Stage 6.47D.9 — Adaptive Magic integration

Gameplay commits 1dab7459ba228ae06aa46e4a3480c8317af8fefa, 0855ad43a889e626d717e98609af523a68a867eb, 5c5f6dd0f09d18d5ad3cc5d60971cb8149666273. Same balance branch; no merge or deploy. All 419 graph nodes are now enabled, including the eight adaptive skills and gates. All cost six points from empty graph in their own mode. Talent cap remains 100.

## Provisional implementation
- Arcane Nova: each actually consumed Arcane Infusion charge splashes 35% of its damage to up to two living active additional enemies. Grave Bloom has priority, no simultaneous Nova on that charge. No proc/extra/recursive/offhand charge consumption, no reserves, no extra status.
- Mana Convergence: funded Arcane cast or funded Infusion activation below 30% Max Mana refunds up to 4 Mana, shared 8s cooldown. No refund per target or charge hit.
- Frost Resonance: paid Frost cast against previously chilled enemy, 25% effective Spell Power to up to two other already chilled active enemies, global 4s cooldown. Glacial Echo: 30% effective Spell Power to the previously chilled primary enemy, global 5s cooldown. Both can seed normal Chill when owned standalone; no new control duration multiplier.
- Controlled Burn: eligible paid primary Burn gains 25% tick potency; existing Fire Spread, Wildfire and Conflagration use 75% of original pre-bonus potency. Standalone adaptive-only Burn is marked primaryOnly and cannot feed either propagation path. Cinder Focus: paid Fire cast against previously burning isolated foe (no reserves), one strongest original active Burn tick, 5s cooldown. Both can seed initial standalone Burn when needed. Existing Fire Spread and Combustion coefficients otherwise unchanged.
- Static Focus: secondary chain and mark damage reduced by 25%, redirect up to that raw amount to primary target, limited to 30% chain base and enemy class caps. No extra jumps/marks. Storm Conductor: isolated paid Lightning cast, 30% effective Spell Power additional damage, 5s cooldown, no reserve enemies. Removed two legacy storm_conductor 20% global multiplier references before enabling the formerly inaccessible ID.
- Default raw adaptive damage caps: 4% normal, 1.5% elite, 0.75% boss Max HP per target/activation; Cinder Focus caps 2.5%/1%/0.5%. Native direct modifiers and Barrier still apply. Integer rounding may affect small values. No new secondary status loops.
- Native simulator rows export magicAdaptiveMetrics. Existing Frost Spread and Shatter effects kept pending matched calibration (no speculative additional buff). Existing core Fire/Lightning chain geometry unchanged.
- Existing mode-switch implementation refunds old element-specific nodes and recursively removes dependent nodes lacking valid alternative routes. Tests cover both refund and independently owned bridge retention. All eight adaptive modes tested against actual talent allocator. New numerical values and tradeoffs remain calibration candidates, not final balance.
