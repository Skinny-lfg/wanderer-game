# Stage 6.16 — Individual Elites, Bosses and normal group sizes (native, no buffs/nerfs)

## Player-directed goal

- Elite benchmark **60–70% player win rate** as a provisional target, subject to earned progression and real equipment validation.
- Boss benchmark must be harder than Elite and account for optional Boss preparation; **no immutable universal target was prescribed**.
- Distinguish whether normal fights against **1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 and 20 real native mobs** have appropriate difficulty.
- Identify which individual native Boss and Elite types are unusually weak/strong, and which **real native solo mob roles and observed names** are similarly misbalanced.
- Compare across E5/E15/E35/E70 controlled point budgets separately, plus six native Affinity Realms.

## New native fight design

Reuse Stage 6.15's **native legal deterministic 6 Realm × 4 Budget × 2 profile** build builder but with **fresh, disjoint 6.16 seed starts**. Each matched scenario executes ten actual native enemy setups: 8 requested-and-observed normal mob counts (1,2,3,4,6,8,12,20), one native randomly generated Elite, and one native true Affinity Boss.

- Pilot 2 new native seeds each cell: 96 matched ten-way configurations, **960 native combat executions**.
- Full 8 new native seeds each cell: 384 matched ten-way configurations, **3,840 actual native combats**. Pilot is the first 2 seeds of the final 8, so **do not add pilot fights as new evidence**.
- Matched setup: same player full rolled gear, same native v15 TalentGraph nodes and elemental attunement, same available/spent budget, identical starting HP/MP and max stats, seed/resolved-seed and native Realm, Risk 1 and starting Condition, native Focus targeting, native Rare equipment.
- Enemy roster/role/HP/ATK/skills is intentionally NOT matched, because **that is the independent native encounter condition**. Starting RNG is held fixed, battle streams may diverge with enemy actions.
- Native solo encounters spawn **Tank, Skirmisher or Caster** depending on native weighted roles; a solo Minion is not generated by the real Stage 8.0 spawn plan. Larger groups contain actual native Minions, with actual group-size scaling. Do not compare raw group number as if each mob were a full-strength Tank.
- Elite types are selected by the real native roster. **No injecting the desired Elite type or artificially changing mob abilities.** Some observed values such as `caster` / `tank` are generic subtypes, not names of dedicated Elite artwork.
- Record per native kind: actual wins, losses, rate, damage to player, duration, observed role composition, per-Boss ID, per-Elite type, solo mob role/name and per-budget/per-Realm breakdown. For tiny per-name solo samples, include denominators and caveat; not enough to declare imbalance conclusively.

## Analysis and decision gates

The 6.15 matrix previously showed 384 matched Normal-3 vs Elite vs Boss trios with 379/384 Normal victories, 346/384 Elite victories and 106/384 Boss victories; do not present this as per-enemy subtypes or mob group-size evidence. The 6.16 cohort is fresh and separately interpretable. If the full study finishes, compare **the observed 6.16 rates** to the provisional Elite target of 60–70%. Individual enemy improvements should be evaluated with matched seeds and per-tier balance, not blind global HP/attack multipliers.

**Limitations:** all Talent points are legal but assigned artificially; native equipment is rolled at Rare, Danger risk only; Enemy Bosses are unprepared first-ring native combat encounters, not naturally reached after 15 Realms, potions, loot or Boss preparation. Native solo names may have small samples. These rates are conditional diagnostic rather than natural account expected win probabilities.

No balance coefficients, Boss/Elite mechanics, saves, production Main, art assets, Firebase or deployment are changed. One scoped PR targeting `audit/v013-stage6` only.
