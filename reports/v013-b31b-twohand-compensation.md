## Experiment
8 fixed legal 30-point E10 panels: Blood/Decay/Fury Bow and Two-Handed, Arcane and Decay Staff. Each keeps same native armor and non-hand rolls, same talent allocation and roster/seed within panel. Same-weapon variants identical stats/gear. One-hand references Sword+Dagger, Dual Daggers, Wand+Orb/Tome are contextual fixed-talent controls, not independently optimized. Native gear same tier and rarity, not equal realized affixes or cost. 20 fresh matched screening seeds per scenario, solo/2/4/12/boss; 5100 fights. Record direct base, raw pre-barrier and effective HP damage per main hit separately from total DPS, secondary damage, status, mana, defense and outcomes. Pair differences and descriptive bootstrap intervals; disclose timeouts and surrender. Instrumentation must reproduce prior native results under identical seeds; no shipped production drift. Test all arms in smoke before launching matrix.
## Decision gate
Seek smallest compensation that materially improves damage per hit and preserves vulnerability and situational identities. No per-build win target or inference of natural global balance. Check DPS versus full loadout reference, especially secondary and DoT amplification and mana constraints. No candidate accepted without separate 50-100 held-out confirmation and user decision. No production code, merging, deployment or scoring changes. Rollback by reverting diagnostic commit.
