# Stage 6.47D.9E — Stat Identity & Affinity Coherence

Design: deterministic per-skill gates, lane/tier, mixed sides and weapon paths replace hash-based minor stat selection. Every original stat slot remains; all 419 IDs and prerequisites preserved. Primary attribute minor nodes removed; gear, base attributes and post-100 attribute allocation remain. Examples: Precision/Brutal Critical/Adrenaline/Sanguine Fury/Crimson Rush/Fervent Life gates are Critical Chance; Guarded Block/Retaliation are Guard; Barrier Pulse/Ward Echo are Barrier Power; Blood Drinker is Healing Power; Efficient Cast is Mana Cost Reduction. Decay physical side gains Attack Power, magical side Spell Power; Aegis has Spell Power.

Guard Chance 1/1.5/2 points, total cap 65%, no shield 35% strength, existing shield reductions retained. Barrier Power 2/3/4% additive with gear wardBonus, only on generation; replaces Starting Barrier minor nodes. Healing Power 1/1.5/2%, combat heals through setHeal (not stored reserve releases); Mana Cost Reduction 1/1.5/2%, additive cap 25%, minimum paid cost 1, two-decimal costs after existing modifiers. No new crit damage or generic status application nodes. Internal block names remain for compatibility; UI uses Guard. Planner heuristic updated.

9D had failed active stat regressions (old flatSpeed assertion, absent Sunder tier) and historical v12 test suite used retired draft APIs. New tests cover actual v15 graph; old suite is not a validation gate. The 9D Vitality E9 timeout is unresolved; repeat natural cohorts and preserve errors. No deployment; calibration pending.

Follow-up gate review: Arcane/Spell Echo scale with spell damage, not critical chance; Retaliation needs Barrier, Stored Force needs Guard/Barrier, Guardian's Oath heals, Fury Recovery uses HP. Sword Sweep/Mace Shockwave gain Attack Power gates; Decay defensive lane preserves Evasion. The original fixed node counts of 9D are superseded by intentional paths.
