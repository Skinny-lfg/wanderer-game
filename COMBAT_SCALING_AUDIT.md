# Combat scaling audit — baseline d48af993

R means JavaScript Math.round. Attack speed below is expressed as seconds per attack; increasing the interval slows the actor. References describe the root gameplay entrypoint.

1. **Level and wave** (combat/expedition-progression.js:14; combat/v012-combat-core.js:16–35). Reference x=E through 5, then 5+log1p((E−5)/5). Raw HP/ATK/DEF = 27+14x, 4+3x, 1+.62x. For stages 1–4: R(R(HP×1.4)×1.4×[1,1.12,1.24,1.48]), R(R(ATK×1.25)×1.4×[1,1.08,1.16,1.25]), R(R(DEF×1.2)×1.4×[1,1.06,1.12,1.22]). Fifth wave on a multiple of ten instead rounds raw ×1.38/1.28/1. Historical fifth-wave rule is based on wave index, independently of the actual Boss flag. The non-progression-module level>200 fallback is not the active HTML path. Intervening rounding prevents folding factors algebraically.
2. **Actor identity** (combat/v012-combat-runtime.js:20–34). Boss doubles each estimate. Otherwise role HP/ATK/DEF factors: Tank 1.32/.90/1.35, Skirmisher .96/1.10/1, Caster .90/1.08/1, Minion .68/.70/.55. Elite substitutes its archetype's hp/atk/def/interval from ELITE_ARCHETYPES. Round each product; floors 1/1/0. Guardian and Elite Tank respectively start with max(3,R(.9x)) and max(1,R(1+.3x)) Barrier on the active path. Base interval Tank/Caster 1.72, Skirmisher 1.42, Minion 1.90, Boss 1.62. Normal is an encounter category, not another role. Missing ordinary role defaults to Skirmisher.
3. **Headcount** (online/v012-expedition-runtime.js:190–205; combat/v012-combat-core.js:40–59; online/v013-enemy-roster.js:77–87). Count after Condition calculation; final Elite/Boss initially alone. One actor bypasses group scaling. Base factors at n=2,3,4: .7/.65, .5/.45, .4/.35. For n>=6: HP=(1.65+.045(n−6))/n, ATK=.16 below 18 else .12. Small-group Minion: HP×.95, ATK×.90, DEF×.35; specialist floors .57/.50 after multiplying each base by 1.35, DEF×.72. Large-group Minion: ×.95/.92, DEF×.25 through 12 else .18. Specialist: HP=min(.38,1.85×base), ATK=min(.32,1.55×base), DEF×.66. Round, clamp HP/ATK >=1 (preserve zero ATK), DEF/baseDef >=0. Without roster DEF factor .45/.22/.15 (<=4/=12/other). Group size 5 has no default budget: do not invent one.
4. **Encounter pressure** (runtime:209–218). For initially spawned actors HP=R(maxhp×endurance×(1+.10risk)), interval×1.15, ATK=max(1,R(atk×pressure×(1+.12risk))). Guarded/Dangerous/Brutal risk=0/1/2. Endurance ordinary/Elite/Boss = 3/3.05/1.8. Pressure ordinary/Boss=.65, Elite Tank=1.40, remaining Elites=1.12. No HP/DEF risk applied to later escorts. The .65 Boss factor is active and intentionally preserved pending design review.
5. **Boss preparation, traits, position and escorts** (runtime:219–244). Activate compatible Boss modifiers before preparation attack R, then apply ordinary trait, then node-2 Vanguard to first group member if group length>1: R(maxhp×1.8), ceil(atk×1.2). Its first-strike combat flag is a separate effect. Boss modifiers: Bone Ward adds max(1,R(.15 maxhp)); Graven Hide rounds DEF and baseDef ×1.12; Retinue adds escorts; Soul Drain affects healing; Frenzy speeds below half; Profane Pulse deals capped-by-barrier periodic player damage. Preparation changes final leader attack or escort count. Escorts are separately generated normal actors, HP=R(HP×1.2), ATK=R(ATK×.35), interval×1.3, without group budget, endurance or trait. Node 2 is an implicit map convention: later replace with authored room identity only after a design decision.
6. **Trait and condition order.** combat/v013-expedition-foundation.js:13–35 owns parameters. online/v013-encounter-traits.js:23–64 applies HP, DEF/baseDef, interval, ATK, barrier then first-strike lead. Armored .92 HP/1.20 DEF; Frenzied .90 HP/1.15 speed; Sigilbound .90 ATK and .12 post-HP barrier; Ambush advances .75s; Regrowth heals R(.02 maxhp) every 5s to R(.10 maxhp); Death Throes R(.02 hero maxhp) per death capped R(.04 hero maxhp). Conditions act after escort creation except Carrion Swarm which changes count first. Relentless Hunt divides interval by 1+.15(1−mitigation), floor .15s. Blighted Ground reduces healing; Ward adds max(1,R(.15 boss maxhp×(1−mitigation))); Rite below half rounds attack ×(1+.12(1−mitigation)), divides interval by 1+.08(1−mitigation); Veiled Passages affects travel. Purifying mitigation .5 else 0.
7. **Field, reserve and combat.** runtime:245–261 attaches role/Elite abilities, seeds clocks from final intervals, takes first four into field, and then applies Elite bounty (ceil HP/ATK ×1.25). Existing reserve actors already have their stats; promotion resets their clock, not their scaling. Summons instead use online/v013-boss-infrastructure.js:33–85, their own hp/atk/interval factors and caps. Do not reuse escort rules for them. In combat, slow/stun/delay and windup alter due time, not base ATK. First eligible visible actor in array order can strike. combat/v012-combat-runtime.js:624–692 uses max(ceil(.24 ATK),R(ATK×(1−min(.75,heroDEF/(heroDEF+1.35ATK))))) before strike bonuses, weaken, guard, avoidance, block, barrier and HP. Player-side Sunder changes current DEF with a floor of R(.25 baseDef); hit paths use effective DEF, damage modifiers, barrier then HP. Direct ability and DoT channels have their own mitigation. Barrier break can change the Guardian cycle. Keep effect-specific rounding in its owning module.
8. **Affinity is dispatch, not a single coefficient.** online/v013-enemy-roster.js:41–65 weights specialists. online/v013-enemy-abilities.js:5–48,69–182 defines Blood (ward, empowered siphon, healing hex), Decay (ward, wound, weakening hex), Vitality (mend, siphon, mend), Aegis (ward, breaker, ward), Fury (rally, two charged strikes, rally), Magic (ward, mana strike, charged spell); minions have family pack passives. Boss effects respectively sacrifice/lifesteal, brood mitigation, death heal, ward cycle, interruptible strike/enrage, and Storm Node/discharge (online/v013-boss-abilities.js:6–144). These change encounter outcomes after spawn; keep them distinct from role budgets.

**Classification.** Role, group size, encounter pressure, risk, trait, condition and skill are distinct active layers. Redundant work is the repeated HP/maxHP assignment and repeated derivation of the same risk factors per actor. Group-size budget is split across two owners. The apparent double 1.4 and post-Boss .65 are not removable identities. No compensatory multiplier is justified. Vanguard location, Boss pressure, Elite Tank pressure and lack of escort risk require balance/design decisions.
