# B7 — Build Identity & Situational Balance

## Scope and design intent
Freeze B6.1 at e29eb23556ffd580f306b684df715d3d7771b222 (successful run 46141656892). No changes to gameplay, talent values, gear, enemies, economy, or agents. The objective is meaningful strengths and weaknesses, not equal win rates: ST should excel in small encounters relative to AoE, AoE should improve its relative advantage as group size increases, burst versus sustained Rot should offer different payoff, Frost should trade raw damage for control, and Arcane should enable amplification. Aegis and Radiance should enable other affinities; their defensive hybrid need not be competitive offensively. Future flavor legendaries remain outside this stage.

## Evidence and sample
Use the B6.1 current artifacts of 24 regular profiles only (not the pre-candidate arms or four experimental Lightning cells), verify exact revision, legal profile, 30-point allocation, native hashes, instrumentation hash and scenario/gear fingerprints. Reuse 9,600 fights. Add three missing normal group sizes (2, 8, 16) for those profiles; add 16 complete profiles, including four explicit same-kit alternatives (Arcane amplification with Sword or Wand; Fury Daggers ST; Blood Two-Handed ST). The four added talent allocations are validated against the current graph. 40 profiles x 11 scenarios x 50 matched seeds = 22,000 total, 12,400 new main fights. No repeat of existing cells. Seven normal sizes (1/2/4/8/12/16/22), tank/caster/vampire elites, rotating boss. Preserve B6.1 seed and realm schedule for all reused scenarios; new sizes append to that schedule. Same-size same-kit comparisons are matched; different sizes do not share identical opponents. Reused data is not independent confirmation. Other B6 profiles and additional elite/boss types remain prior/contextual evidence only.

## Comparisons and outputs
18 explicit same-kit comparisons: Fury ST/AoE Daggers, Blood ST/AoE Two-Handed, Arcane amplification versus Nova with physical/caster kits, Fire versus Lightning with Wand/Staff, Arcane versus Frost, Cycle versus Plagueheart with three weapons, Decay + Fury, three Blood support hybrids, Arcane + Aegis, Radiance + Arcane, Grave Bloom and Radiance + Aegis. Compare packages with the same point budget, not pure effects or optimal human builds. Report group-size curves for win rate, DPS, secondary and periodic damage share, first kill, median winning time, HP, Mana, recovery, absorption, resource starvation, control, actor exposure, native telemetry, and paired deltas. Compare time-to-win only among joint wins for paired timing. Relative AoE advantage shift = mean paired win delta at groups 8/12/16/22 minus solo delta; inspect DPS, timing and ceiling effects alongside it. No arbitrary single power score; no claim that group-size curve is causal for identical enemy composition. Approximate exploratory intervals are not multiple-testing adjusted.

## Quality gate
Native regression, legal build/kit and smoke checks before matrix. Fail aggregate if source, profile, seed, scenario, gear fingerprints or coverage differ. Existing conservative surrender policy only; disclose all timeouts and unresolved bounds. No silent discard. Diagnose whether roles have a meaningful niche or are dominated across damage, clear time, survival and resources; distinguish intended defensive tradeoffs from failures. No automated nerf/buff, agent retune, merge or deploy. Any future numerical candidate requires explicit evidence and independent seeds. B8 named elite/boss calibration and natural progression remain separate. Natural aggregate target policy remains 85% normals / 70% elites / 50% bosses, not controlled per-build quotas.
