# B7.1 — Sustained Rot Identity

Baseline 31b0cb8df01e70c442e9611771992d0061de162c, validated B7. User requested testing the steady all-target option and a rarer, more expensive infection-wide proc that seeds 1–2 Rot on the selected target. The precise probability/cost/bonus of the latter are experimental design choices, not approved gameplay. No healing.

## Arms (test-only native VM overrides)
- Current: 45% per infected target tick, +200% tick damage, 2+2 Mana/stack, one successful global activation/tick.
- Uncapped diagnostic: same chance, bonus, cost, reserve, but independent activation per infected active enemy tick. Isolates removal of the shared cap.
- Steady: 100% chance per infected active enemy tick, +75% tick damage, 1 Mana/stack, no global cap.
- Outbreak: one roll per global periodic second if at least one living active enemy is infected. 25% chance, 16 Mana flat, one payment. Applies 1 or 2 persistent ability-origin Rot stacks (equal probability) using current native Rot potency to the selected living target, including an initially clean target if another enemy was infected. Then all infected active enemies receive +200% of their own base Rot on that tick, including the new stacks. No extra ticks, no recursion, no reserve enemies or arena nodes, no stack consumption. Native cap of six retained. No reroll after failure in same tick. Does not grant health. All arms retain 15% max Mana reserve and pure Decay eligibility. Native periodic Rot already ignores enemy Barrier; no change to damage routing. Costs quoted before existing modifiers. Sequential spending in Uncapped and Steady can still prefer earlier targets when Mana is low.

## Study design
Ten legal 30-point E10 profiles: six pure Decay Plagueheart weapon packages plus Sword/Shield/Leather and Wand/Tome/Plate variants, with matched non-armor gear and identical talents. Two unaffected controls: pure Cycle/Sword and Decay+Fury/Daggers. Armor substitution tests package survivability and offensive/resource tradeoffs, not defense alone. Rare gear, fixed mechanics target policy, no runes, contracts or boons. Four arms, seven scenarios (normal 1/4/12/22, Tank elite, Aegis boss separately and five other boss affinities), 50 new matched seeds, 14,000 fights. Compare win rates, DPS, periodic/secondary DPS, HP, Mana, procs, seeded stacks, average stacks per tick, six-stack exposure, time to kill and armor contrasts. Report candidate-only counters separately, raw bonus before multipliers is not effective HP damage. Same encounter fingerprints and exact within-profile gear required; non-armor fingerprints matched for armor comparisons. Deterministic fixtures cover cap, reserve, hybrid exclusion, simultaneous targets, proc chance thresholds, one payment, one/two stacks and native Barrier bypass. Regression and smoke before full matrix.
