# B6.1 — Targeted Power Redistribution

## Decision and intent
Baseline e136de0360baeb5d36a033fe160afadce02af28b (B6, run 46139710160, summary artifact 15261537789). User wants Arcane (unattuned/Arcane) to specialize in buffs and amplification, and Aegis/Radiance to enable other Affinities; low offensive performance of their combination is not a defect. Potential future legendary items may give defensive hybrids distinct flavor. Preserve both defensive trees and their hybrid; no forced equal win rates. Keep existing Arcane enhancement and resource abilities unchanged in this candidate; a more substantial buff identity redesign is a separate design decision, not an unapproved implementation.

## Candidate coefficients (provisional)
- Grave Bloom: 2 -> 1 new Rot per paid Infusion hit; burst from 100% to 75% of total active tick damage. Preserve 2 existing stacks spread to up to 2 living targets at 75% potency, class caps, charge/cost, stack lifecycle, and preparation. B6 hybrid has 99.5% wins, 110.84 DPS, 41.12 periodic DPS; native metrics show 3696 seeded stacks, 1848 activations and 63114 burst damage. Reduce ST and automatic seed throughput while preserving spreading identity.
- Fire Kindling: per-tick effective SP coefficient .15 -> .12 before existing 15% potency per rank. Preserve Wildfire, Controlled Burn, costs, and native direct casts. Wand Fire: 121.92 DPS, of which 47.95 periodic, 99% wins. Aim to reduce Fire periodic power without globally penalizing spellcasting.
- Lightning: Forked no longer grants free Overcharge rank 1 (60%); standalone weak chain scale 30%, with Overcharge ranks 60/70/80/90/100%. First five chain factors identical regardless of Forked; sixth remains .15. Marks scale with Overcharge (standalone .30). Lightning Infusion damage coefficient reduced 20% without altering normal funded attacks, mana, chain count or base SP. Preserve AoE identity, improve rank incentive and reduce excess primary damage without blanket nerf. 
- Plagueheart: chance 30% -> 45% on Rot ticks, keeping 200% extra damage, 2+2 Mana per stack, one global proc per tick, 15% reserve and pure Decay eligibility. Wand/Staff focus win 51.25%/50.5%, mana remaining 53.04%/56.60%; low sustained throughput, not proven mana shortage. Keep Corruption Cycle, Rot base and hybrids unchanged.

No changes to Aegis, Radiance, Frost, Blood, Fury, Arcane-mode stat buffs, gear, loot, enemies, economy, agents, or talent acquisition paths. Source and docs mirrored. Stable IDs preserved. No new legendary content.

## Validation
28 fixed legal 30-point E10 profiles: 6 Fire/Lightning weapon variants, 6 Plagueheart weapon variants, Grave Bloom hybrid, 4 equal-cost B5 Lightning factorial cells, 11 unaffected controls (including Radiance/Aegis, Arcane support hybrids, Frost and Cycle). 50 new seeds per eight scenarios (normal 1/4/12/22, tank/caster/vampire elite, rotating boss). 11,200 paired blocks, 22,400 main fights. Native runtime fingerprints and opponent/gear parity; existing opt-in solo-normal stalemate research policy. Compare per-profile and scenario win changes, DPS, periodic/secondary damage, mana, HP, time-to-win, and native activation counts. All 11 unaffected controls must be byte-equivalent in recorded fight rows. Interpret controlled build packages separately from earned progression.

Acceptance: directional redistribution without eliminating a useful niche, better Plagueheart throughput without unbounded mana/procs, restored Overcharge rank value, no unintended cross-affinity drift. No universal per-build win target; natural aggregate remains 85/70/50 normal/elite/boss. Regression, smoke and matched results precede decision. Reject unexplained timeouts/mismatches. No automatic coefficient changes or merge/deploy. Revert this candidate commit if needed. Natural progression and broader B7 matchup calibration remain pending.
