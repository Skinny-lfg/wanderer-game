# B4.3 — Targeted Ability Corrections

Approved candidates after B4.2 (run 46129586647; 18,800 fights; zero timeouts). Baseline d38bc6a14bfc912881ab09fa51b98267a1c7a684. This is a candidate, not final balance approval.

- Aegis starting Barrier: 5% HP + 8% SP -> 10% HP + 16% SP. Barrier Mastery 20% -> 30% per rank, multiplicative with the base capacity. No periodic regeneration or change to source caps, Ward Echo, Bastion, Runic Shield, Stored Force, Retaliation, or Steadfast. Ward Strength applied once. Expected rank-five base capacity is 2.5x B4.2, not a 2.5x win-rate prediction.
- Terminal Decay: old +15% at six stacks -> +3% per active stack capped at six (+18%). Only the existing periodic tick multiplier changes; Incubation and Plagueheart retain their existing placement in the tick formula. No change to burst consumption, stack lifecycle, or mana costs. Stable legacy ID retained for compatibility.
- Deep Winter: base Frost stays +22% attack interval. Ranks 1–5 change from +22/26/30/34/38% to +30/34/38/42/46%. Durations, stack threshold, immunity and boss resistance unchanged. This is interval increase, not direct attacks-per-second reduction.
- Red Guillotine: no coefficient change; test identical legal Blood/Fury crit-priority and speed build with Rupture, prepared vs unprepared. Record actual entry crit and speed.
- Frost Resonance and Winter Field: no coefficient change. Equal-cost 2x2 factorial (neither, A, B, both) with shared prerequisites and explicit legal stat alternatives. Same factorial for Terminal Decay and Corruption Cycle, both before and after the patch.
- Frozen unrelated gameplay, economy and agent policies. Lightning control included. Twelve configurations, four E10 matchups, 50 new matched seeds per configuration/scenario; synthetic Rare equipment, 30-point allocation (29/30 marginal for individual purchases), no runes. Raw rows and matched gear/roster fingerprints retained. Approximate exploratory intervals and factorial contrasts; no natural progression claims. Stop on errors, invalid allocations, drift, mismatched roster/gear, or timeout.
- Verify direction of Aegis survival recovery without runaway absorption; compare Ward Shield/Orb and hybrids. Verify Terminal Decay ticks at every stack count, marginal benefit with/without Cycle; verify Chill frequency and utility while preserving Frost identity. Review Red crit and Frost interaction before proposing additional changes. Compare against original B4/B4.2 separately; not identical seeds across stages.
- Regression and smoke gates precede simulations. Full results must be inspected before accepting the coefficients. Rollback: revert candidate commit, no merge/deploy. Natural progression, browser parity and cumulative power budget remain subsequent gates.
