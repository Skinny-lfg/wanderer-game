# B4.1 — Ability & Affinity Identity Rework

Baseline: B4, e2c10733794bad2bcadcc66ae6d01bd9cf68ce8c. Candidate mechanics, not final balance approval.

- Red Guillotine: physical main-hand crit, 8% pre-critical base hit per active Bleed stack, maximum 80%, 5s cooldown, no stack consumption.
- Terminal Decay: +15% Rot tick at six stacks; no extended secondary duration.
- Lightning: Overcharge scales chain factors 60–100% across ranks; Forked Lightning adds sixth jump and Static Marks; Chain Discharge owns the four-paid-cast discharge (56% SP, cap 3.5% enemy HP). Static Focus and Storm Conductor unchanged.
- Barrier Mastery: +20% initial Aegis Barrier per rank. Source-specific capacity, not unbounded accumulation; general recurring grants share a 10% HP + 10% SP capacity, with Barrier Power/Ward Strength applied once. Orb and Runic Shield replenish their own pools. Ward Echo reduces Barrier depletion by 15%, independent of weapon. Steadfast Guard halves overflow on the first breaking hit per encounter. Arcane Bastion grants 30% mana discount on the next funded cast/Infusion after absorption, 6s cooldown; no damage or extra Barrier.
- Frost: paid casts apply Frost, five stacks freeze (normal 1.5s, elite .75s; boss basic attack delay .75s); immunity follows (4s normal/elite, 6s boss). Stacks expire after 8s. Deep Winter improves Chill, Winter Field spreads stacks, Frost Resonance spreads once on Freeze, Glacial Echo adds an isolated-target stack, Shatter damages at the end of Freeze. Shatter 45% effective SP, class HP caps 5/2.5/1.5%.
- Stable save keys barrier_pulse and lingering_rot are retained for the replacement talents. No duplicate nodes or old effects. Historical reports and baseline scripts remain historical, not specifications for the new build.
- Agent strategy labels/classifications reflect changed mechanics, without a new optimization pass.
- CI: focused identity regression and full npm test. No natural progression balance approval in this stage.
