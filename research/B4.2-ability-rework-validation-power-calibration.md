## Hypotheses / decision gate
1. Red Guillotine contributes beyond Rupture without consuming Bleeds. Terminal Decay contributes only with six stacks and remains a situational investment.
2. Lightning lower early chain power, meaningful late ranks, Forked owns marks, Chain Discharge owns paid-cast burst; avoid overcorrecting single target.
3. Aegis bounded protection scales with attributes, Ward Echo valuable without Orb, Steadfast protects breaking overflow, Bastion increases funded-action efficiency. Confirm whether survival loss is acceptable rather than blindly restore free Barrier.
4. Frost trades damage for meaningful control and reduced attack frequency, with bosses resistant and no permanent Freeze. Investigate activation or eligibility defects before tuning.
5. Inspect scenario-specific marginal and equal-cost effects, cumulative power change vs B4, resource saturation, unintended damage/defense regressions, and timeout/denominator integrity. Exploratory intervals are not multiple-testing-adjusted or held-out confirmation. Flag meaningful changes (e.g. >=10 percentage points or >=15% DPS/attack-rate shift) for investigation, not automatic acceptance/nerf. No natural win-rate target imposed on controlled builds. Full interaction/affinity budget, natural progression, browser parity and independent confirmation remain later gates.
6. Stop on failed matching, invalid allocation, runtime drift, timeout, or missing artifact. Preserve evidence; correct demonstrated defects separately. Revert only B4.2 research harness/workflow if needed. No merge/deploy. User approval required before numerical tuning.
