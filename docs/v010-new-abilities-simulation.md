# Wanderer v0.10 — new ability design and offline A/B

**Decision: IMPLEMENT WITH CHANGES for an experimental Shatter + Double Strike prototype only. No gameplay change was made.** The recommended *next* configuration is Shatter after three successful mana-funded Frost spell hits against a Chilled enemy, dealing **30% Spell Power** once per **6 seconds**; and main-hand Daggers **25%** chance for one extra **55% normal pre-critical hit**. That extra hit has no critical roll, affinity application, Legendary trigger, off-hand trigger or recursion. Keep existing Frost set tiers and the automatic Off-Hand Dagger attack. Neither ability should go live until its UI explanation and stronger late-game acquired-gear validation are reviewed. Fire and Staff should remain at the current balance for now.

## Evidence and Legendary audit

The simulator loads the actual `combat/legendary.js` file: **22 catalog identities**, **19 defined signature abilities**, and three traditional/generic identities (`grasp-of-the-sunbound`, `scepter-of-the-emberstar`, `the-eternal-oath`). All 19 definitions retain their saved affinity and cooldown. Six specifically requested abilities were tested on identical Legendary equipment with their signature enabled and disabled, 80 paired trials each at D100; the prototype interaction check used 100 paired trials each. Their triggers fired and the disabled versions did not fire. Winterheart's own delay was especially material (baseline 51/80 wins enabled vs 20/80 disabled). Ashen Seal, Kindled Heart, Spell Echo, Toxic Fang and Crimson Crescent also fired, though several pairs were at a win/loss ceiling, so contribution cannot be estimated reliably from win rate alone. Detailed proc, HP and outcome aggregates are in the JSON artifact.

The previous v0.10 harness **loaded** Legendary definitions, but the earned milestone snapshots contained zero Winterheart, Tome, Starweaver or Crescent, seven Pendant snapshots and two Fang snapshots. Thus earlier results did **not** adequately exercise most requested signature powers. Winterheart remains intact and Shatter adds damage rather than another delay. Ashen Seal and Kindled Heart were checked with Ignition; the Fire proposal is not selected. Toxic Fang and Crimson Crescent keep their original affinity application and off-hand behavior; Double Strike does not re-trigger either ability. **Starweaver Relic is a Wand, not a Staff**, and **Tome of the Eternal Flame cannot be paired with two-handed Staff** under current rules. Spell Echo remains a Wand-specific Legendary interaction, not evidence for global Staff tuning.

## Combat coverage and limits

Screening used 32 paired trials per candidate at D40/D100. Twelve selected alternatives received **100 or 120 paired production-combat trials per dungeon** at D20, D40, D60, D80, D100, D120, D160 and D200. Normal D99/D199 and boss D100/D200 expeditions were checked separately with 100 trials per build. All four elites (Tank, Berserker, Vampire, Caster) and four dungeon themes were rotated. The JSON reports Wilson 95% win intervals, remaining HP, time, damage in/out, DoT, mana spent, player and enemy attack counts, affinity applications, extra strikes, Legendary triggers and control delays. It includes legal 2/3/5-piece affinity fixtures built from acquired stat distributions while preserving Legendary identity affinities. Dark, Regen and Shield controls at D100/D200 had identical outcomes under the selected pair.

Original earned snapshots were used where present; generated mixed-tier, mixed-rarity **legal** fixtures are labeled separately. Late Frost and Daggers are largely generated because no suitable acquired D160/D200 inventory existed. Those results show mechanical potential, **not** measured late-game player viability. Generated D100/D120 samples sometimes had near-zero baseline wins; avoid reading large relative percentages as a balanced progression outcome. Normal D99/D199 checks reuse D100/D200 equipment snapshots (or labeled generated fixtures). Shatter and Double Strike do not heal, extend Chill, delay bosses, or change the existing two-handed and Off-Hand Dagger restrictions. Double Strike is limited to at most one extra hit per main attack, while existing attack-speed diminishing returns bound the underlying rate; it still needs a high-speed regression check before implementation.

## Persistent D1–D200 progression

The existing production-state simulator ran **20 Casual, 20 Experienced and 20 Speed-oriented players** for each configuration, with matched starting seeds and up to 600 expeditions per player. It keeps loot and rune acquisition, Forge, Merchant, protection, death loss, farming, Gold and Fragments. Median values are per 20-player policy group.

| Configuration | D200 Casual / Experienced / Speed | Total | Median dungeon C / E / S | Median deaths C / E / S | Median farm runs C / E / S |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Current v0.9.3/v0.10 baseline | 0 / 3 / 5 | **8/60** | 39 / 84 / 59 | 3 / 8 / 35 | 456 / 413 / 451 |
| Shatter 30% + Double Strike B (recommended experiment) | 0 / 4 / 5 | **9/60** | 50 / 81 / 70 | 3 / 6 / 42 | 449 / 409 / 447 |
| Above + Ignition 4% | 0 / 3 / 4 | **7/60** | 49 / 79 / 134 | 3 / 7 / 42 | 464 / 409 / 450 |
| Shatter 30% + Ignition 4% + Double Strike A | 0 / 5 / 5 | **10/60** | 39 / 79 / 129 | 4 / 7 / 32 | 460 / 409 / 387 |

For the recommended pair, the four Experienced completers reached D200 in 411–552 expeditions; five Speed completers needed 485–577. Median crafts C/E/S were 75/385/367, compared with 75/380/361 at baseline. Median Fragments spent were 19,150/127,560/147,260 vs 16,260/120,070/106,610, and median Gold spent were 90/140/529 vs 126/140/529. These are **model policies**, not human playtime. More importantly, adding a random extra strike changes later RNG consumption, loot and equipment choices, so matched starting seeds are not exact common-random-number counterfactuals. The small 60-player sample cannot prove a 1-player completion difference. No negative currency or invalid unlock appeared. The recommended pair did **not** make D200 completion common, and Staff/Dark/Regen/Shield were not directly empowered. It should remain an experiment, not a release approval.

All candidate mechanics are isolated VM transforms; **production gameplay, Firebase, main, and real saves are unchanged**.

Before implementing either selected ability, gather acquired Frost/Daggers inventories at D100–D200, check boss phases and high attack-speed gear with real Legendary identities, and design English combat log/tooltips. The user should review that evidence and the UI text before any gameplay implementation or publication.
