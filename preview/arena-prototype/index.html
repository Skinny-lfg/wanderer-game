How it works

Enemies arrive at different intervals and move towards the Wanderer. Nearby enemies attack automatically; the encounter ends when every enemy is defeated or the Wanderer falls.

Compare builds

Single Target deals stronger damage to one enemy. Area Damage strikes several nearby enemies for reduced damage. Change the encounter and build, then restart to compare.

Prototype only · balancing, art scale and enemy movement are experimental. No items, XP or account data are changed.
