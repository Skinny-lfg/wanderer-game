## Execution path

index.html:1–7 installs browser globals before the inline application. Account bootstrap loads at 2483. Equipment and talent state feed totalStats (index.html:408); breakdown is a separate UI consumer at 1418. Controller startDungeon (online/v012-expedition-controller.js:98), chooseRealm (156), contract/boon decisions (163–210), travel and visitRoom (241–305) establish the selected encounter. Travel charges already-cleared segments and condition surcharge at 282. enter at 338 gates pending choices. Launch snapshots entry state, persists it and carries capped HP/MP into the encounter (online/v012-expedition-runtime.js:164–181). Wave construction fills at most four visible slots; reserve enemies enter vacated slots. On death the runtime resolves trait pulse before declaring victory, applies boss/summon and spread hooks, and retains the current living target before picking a replacement (265–295). This fallback matters for player control; simulator target policies are separate.

Settlement uses the encounter leader even if an escort dies last, guards against repeat awards, credits resources and rolls loot (295–367). Controller waveWon (349–365) records cleared nodes/history, makes ordinary rewards pending or sets finished and unlocks the next Expedition. Defeat and abandonment instead set failed (366–378). Runtime death, abandon, summary and exit handlers are at 368–395,424–462. Persistence covers session and profile separately: removing migrations must preserve checkpoint restoration and award idempotency.

## Combat and equipment arithmetic

- Enemy baseline: combat/v012-combat-runtime.js:20–34. Relative HP/attack/defense for minion .68/.70/.55, Tank 1.32/.90/1.35, Skirmisher .96/1.10/1, Caster .90/1.08/1. Boss x2 before encounter adjustments; Elite uses its archetype. Attack type also depends on boss/elite identity. Initial interval in seconds: minion 1.90, Skirmisher 1.42, Tank/Caster 1.72, default 1.62, Elite table override. Guardian and Elite Tank can start with barrier.
- Groups of six or more: base HP fraction (1.65+.045*(n-6))/n, attack fraction .16 below 18 or .12 from 18. online/v013-enemy-roster.js:77–87 adjusts those fractions for minions versus specialists and sets defense fractions. For larger groups specialist HP/attack capped at .38/.32. Role composition is seeded and reserves are predominantly minions (49–65).
- All initial actors then receive endurance (Boss 1.8, Elite 3.05, ordinary 3), HP risk x(1+.10*risk), interval x1.15, attack risk x(1+.12*risk) and attack factor (Elite Tank 1.40, other Elite 1.12, else .65). Rounding happens between layers. Preparation can weaken Boss attacks. Traits apply to ordinary groups; escorts added afterwards receive HP x1.2, attack x.35, interval x1.3. Conditions apply after escort creation. Ordinary ability attachment excludes Boss rooms. online/v012-expedition-runtime.js:190–249.
- Conditions affect more than stats: travel, count, encounter state, healing and timed boss Rite are separate entrypoints (online/v013-expedition-conditions.js:42–99). Purifying sets mitigation .5 before count selection. Death Throes can kill the player on an otherwise winning blow (online/v012-expedition-runtime.js:269–278).
- Player: combat/v012-gear-stats.js:28–83. Base attributes 5; two-handed main suppresses offhand. Offhand dagger contributes half offensive fields. Item buff x1.10 only on matching item; rune boost x1.20, one equipped rune per family. Stored weapon effect/magic fields cleared in combat projection. HP=round(55+12 END+flat); attack=round(4+1.8 STR+.4 AGI+flat); defense=round(1+.8 END+flat). Crit scales with CHA, weapon tier and speed; cap 55. Shield block caps at 48, reduction .67 with mace, .62 sword, .58 otherwise. Staff/Wand enable caster resources; Magic and Decay can grant physical builds mana. Permanent talents then buildcraft then Expedition modifiers then attack speed cap 4.2 and power recomputation. Power is a derived score.
- Status processing, barrier/heal, attack triggers, legendary readiness and affinity application occupy combat/v012-combat-runtime.js:36–335. Separate per-target stacks and legacy aggregate DoT fields are synchronized; removing aggregates requires verifying render and checkpoint readers. A rune signature checks slot zero at 335, independently of general equipped rune stat aggregation.
- Potions: stock validation and loadout selection (online/v012-consumables.js:9–12), scouting and map interaction (controller:211–240), effects consumed by stat pipeline and encounter mitigation. Loot: group gold/fragment factor 1/1.1/1.2/1.3 by count times risk; bounty x1.5, trait fragments x1.10. Dungeon gear drop capped at .95 even after fortune x1.25. Distinguish resource award and equipment roll. online/v012-expedition-runtime.js:300–320. Recipe unlock, price, focus, funds and generation are distinct steps in economy/v013-crafting.js:49–72.

## Simulator scope

scripts/simulate-progression.cjs:150–188 creates controlled equipment/build/cards. Lines 189–225 select targets and advance native combat with a tick limit. Lines 226–245 choose route/events; 245–360 distinguish injected encounter and dungeon scenarios. tests/v012-economy-harness.cjs:6–34 substitutes seeded RNG, inert DOM, storage, timers and reward UI; 119–122 loads native modules and replaces bootstrap. This shares combat functions, not browser event timing, player intentions, authentication or natural progression. Fixed-build samples cannot establish full campaign difficulty. No runtime parity or distributional claim is made.

## Limits

The traced paths establish the rules above. Visibility of every passive in every UI, all possible dynamic callbacks, all per-affinity ability combinations and equivalence of every damage branch have not been proven. Tank/Skirmisher/Caster equivalence and Boss/Elite threat ordering remain design targets, not static conclusions. Historical scripts, tests and global exports must be checked against CI and other entrypoints before removal.

## Abilities, UI and execution boundaries

The enemy inspector uses the same normal ability description, Elite definition and Boss profile as the encounter modules (index.html:1024,1033–1039). Normal abilities dispatch by family and role (online/v013-enemy-abilities.js:50–69,113–167); status at 173–181 intentionally returns null for minions after testing Guarding, while describe at 182–186 still returns their family passive. A missing casting badge therefore does not mean an inactive minion. Elite definitions and attach at online/v013-elite-abilities.js:4–14 cover Gravecaller, Rotbound Exarch and Ashen Executioner; deathbound stacking, interrupts, tick, empowered strike and status have distinct consumers at 33–49,68–141. Do not infer all Elites have a Stage 8.2 special.

All six boss profiles provide inspector prose (online/v013-boss-abilities.js:6–16). Blood Offering uses actor age and recovery; Carrion counts living minions for mitigation; Heartroot uses the death callback; Guardian checks existing barrier before a ward; Fury and Magic use charge/interrupt events; Storm Node death alters later casts (32–77,77–143). CONFIRMED UI omission: Guardian description promises a ward every 12 seconds whereas the tick requires no current barrier and an expired vulnerability window. Arcane Convergence prose describes the channel but omits damage basis: tick takes 7% of hero max HP adjusted by Boon, then consumes barrier. Correct text without changing rules. Inline numerical prose is a drift risk; use local definition parameters for repeated thresholds, durations and percentages. Visibility here means a code path produces text, not that a browser layout test was performed.

## Runtime, simulator and tests: parity verdict

Exact whole-game parity is **disproved**. Native combat step reuse is confirmed: scripts/simulate-progression.cjs:218–221 calls harness.tick, tests/v012-economy-harness.cjs:66 calls updateFight, combat/v012-combat-runtime.js:505–513 advances .11 seconds. The CLI independently converts tick count to seconds at line 223 (search ticks*.11); use elapsed native combat time. Native damage reuse does not imply equivalent fight outcomes for unequal RNG streams, target choices, synthetic loadouts, route or timing. The harness replaces bootstrap, account/UI, timers, logging and reward prompts (6–34,119–122); it is not a second implementation of all combat. Keep deterministic external test expectations independent of production arithmetic. Compare controlled decisions, native state after each tick and settlement before using results for balance.

Browser tags absent from the harness loader (static list comparison, query strings stripped): combat/action-bar.js, combat/arena-hud.js, economy/v013-tier-upgrade.js, online/v013-map-fog.js, online/v013-combat-links.js, online/v012-gothic-panels.js, online/v012-auth-bootstrap.js, online/v012-account.mjs, online/v012-feedback.mjs, online/v012-leaderboard-ui.mjs, online/v013-tier-upgrade-ui.js. An omission is a coverage boundary, not proof of a combat discrepancy.
